Meet the Team

Silaw Dicke

Class: Sophmore
Major: Games & Simulations / Minor: Linguistics
Favorite place in Oxford: Ramen Hachi / On campus: The Makerspace in King Library
Favorite memory in Oxford: "I have quite a few great memories in Oxford, on- and off-campus. One that comes to mind, and relates to my favorite restaurant, is when I went with my fellow Kababayan (Miami’s Filipino Student Association) members to Ramen Hachi! It was one of my first times there, and their boba milk tea is sooooo good (I recommend the taro flavor). It was just a lot of fun to hang out with people I normally don’t see outside our club. Another great set of memories are the times I’ve been to Sushi King with my friend group. It’s our main way to get the whole group together and eat some good food. I love their spicy shrimp roll and their orange chicken. Miso soup is a must! A good on-campus memory, though, is when I performed at the Asian American Association’s Fusion, a multicultural performance showcase putting the spotlight on Asian cultures (not just East Asia, either). I performed a traditional dance from the Philippines called tinikling, which we practiced for months beforehand. Getting to perform it was amazing and I can’t wait to do it again!"

Me and my team made a “Swarm” themed game in which you would play as a man on the beach and the goal was to escape the beach while it slowly swarms with krabs.
