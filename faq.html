Frequently Asked Questions

Bar 1868

Do you have to be 21 to enter?

Bar 1868 is currently 21+, except for certain special events.

Does Bar 1868 have food?

It offers snacks such as Grippo's and Easy Mac.

What activities or events are there?

Bar 1868 has pool tables and hosts events, including 18+ drag shows during the academic year.

Peffer Park

What can you do at Peffer Park?

Popular activities include hiking, mountain biking, birding, and other outdoor recreation.

Are dogs allowed?

No, dogs are not allowed at Peffer Park.

When does the park close?

The park closes at dark.

Miami University Disc Golf Course

Is the course free?

Yes, the course is free to play.

How many holes are there?

There are 18 holes.

Where does the course start?

It starts near the Richard and Carole Cocks Art Museum on Miami's Western Campus.

Spring Street Treats

What are the hours?

For the 2026 season, it is open every day from 2–10 p.m. through October 31.

What do they serve?

They have soft serve, smoothies, shaved ice, hot dogs, pretzels, nachos, funnel cakes, and other snacks.

Do they have lactose-free options?

Yes. Their soft serve is lactose-free and also gluten-free.

Puzzle & Putt Oxford

What can you do there?

Puzzle & Putt has three themed escape rooms and a 9-hole indoor mini golf course.

How much is mini golf?

Mini golf is $10 per person per round.

Do you need a reservation?

You don't need a reservation for mini golf—you can walk in during open hours. Escape rooms can be booked online.
