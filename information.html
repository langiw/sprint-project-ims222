Oxford Hidden Gems

Here's a list of the Top 5 Underrated Places to visit in Oxford, Ohio!

Puzzle & Putt

Mini Golf and Escape Rooms

"The escape room was top notch. The props were great quality and the puzzles were unique. The putt-putt course is a little tight but the environment is cool! Worth a look!"

Spring Street Treats

Ice Cream, Dessert, Snacks

"Love their ice cream!! It really is the best without the lactose!! They are so sweet there too. Will let you try their new flavors. They have games and activities for families to play too."

Peffer Park

A Recreational Park On Campus with Trails and a Playground

"A beautiful place with peaceful and quiet nature and places to have outdoor grills and fun."

Bar 1868

A Bar In Uptown—Shows, Events, Food & Drink, Cards, Pool

"Consistently a fun atmosphere to be in. Unique events and a fun variety of drinks keep this underground bar a town gem."

Disc Golf Course

The Miami University Disc Golf Course, Located Around Western Campus

"A bit overgrown in certain areas, but it's a great way to get some movement in while having fun!"
